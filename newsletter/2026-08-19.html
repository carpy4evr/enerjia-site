A Saturday, fully assembled

The Enerjia weekly — everything below is verified with the people who run it.

For once the pitch isn't one great pick. It's that this Saturday, August 22, you could run the whole day without a single reservation:

9:30 AM — a 5k on JFK Promenade. Founder Run Club's birthday run through Golden Gate Park. All paces, zero membership. (Or Marina Green at 10 for Girls Run + Brunch — run, then a table you didn't have to book.)

11 AM — free yoga in the meadow. Whale's Tail Meadow, near the rose garden. Free, donations welcome, bring a mat and layers.

1–6 PM — an inaugural block party. The Guardsmen close Chestnut Street between Pierce & Divisadero for their first-ever Marina Block Party: two live bands, family-friendly, free. First editions become traditions — be able to say you were at this one.

Evening — pick your energy. Create for Good at the Palace of Fine Arts (5:30, making things for good causes), or rest up, because —

Sunday, noon — swing in the park. Lindy in the Park, every Sunday since 1996. Free lesson at noon by the de Young. Thirty years of proof that dancing with strangers works.

SF quietly became a dance floor

New on the map this week — nearly one dance night per night:

  • Wednesdays — Swing Night at DecoDance: free beginner class at 7, in an

    • art-deco cocktail bar on Polk St

  • Select Fridays — Moonlit Moves west coast swing at the Polish Club:

    • classes 8:15, party to midnight

  • Sundays — Lindy in the Park, free, since 1996
  • September 3 — Yerba Buena Salsa Nights on the Gardens esplanade

None need a partner. All of them expect you to come alone and leave otherwise.

Every listing above was checked against the organizer this week. Plans change — tap through to the source before you head out.
