The Singles Circuit

Not a dating app. No profiles to swipe, no openers to agonize over. Just the city's actual calendar of places where talking to strangers is the point — and a room of other single people going.

Get the weekly email

The premise

Every couple you know that met "organically" met at something — a run club, a trivia table, a dance floor, a drawing session. The apps didn't kill those places; they just made them easier to skip. The Circuit is the un-skip: a weekly rotation of events where showing up solo is normal and conversation has a built-in subject.

When you join an event on the Circuit, your chat room is other Circuit people going. Nobody is matched with anybody. You just know you won't be the only one who came hoping to meet someone.

A week on the Circuit

Tuesdays · evenings · citywide

Trivia nights

Dozens of bar quizzes across the city. "Anyone want to make a team?" is the entire social protocol, pre-installed.

Weekday mornings + evenings

Run clubs

Marina Green, Kezar, NOPA — huge groups, all paces, zero membership. The most reliable strangers-to-friends pipeline in SF.

Fridays · 9 PM · Ferry Building

Friday Night Skate

A ten-mile rolling party, every Friday since 1989. Try being a stranger for more than two miles of it.

What it isn't

No swiping, no matching, no DMs from people you never met. If two people leave a trivia night with plans, the Circuit did its job — but the job was always just getting everyone in the same room.
