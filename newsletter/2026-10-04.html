The loudest week of the year

The Enerjia weekly — everything below is verified with the people who run it.

Fleet Week is here. The Blue Angels fly Friday, Saturday and Sunday, noon to four, and it's free from Marina Green — the whole northern waterfront turns into one long picnic where nobody is with everybody they came with. Saturday and Sunday, Fleet Fest takes over Pier 27 and Fisherman's Wharf (10–7 Saturday, 10–5 Sunday): ships to board, live music, a beer garden.

Every pin below has a chat. Say hi in it before you leave the house — "anyone watching from the Green? I'll be by the yacht club gate at noon" — and walk in with people you've already met. That's the whole trick.

Where to hear yourself think

Tuesday — Free First Tuesday. The Legion of Honor, the de Young, and the Conservatory of Flowers, all free, all day. Going to a museum alone has always been normal; this is the day it's also free.

Thursday, 12:30 — a free lunch concert. Quinteto Latino on the Yerba Buena Gardens esplanade. Forty-five minutes of woodwinds and sun; back at your desk by two.

Thursday, 6–10 — Exploratorium After Dark. The weekly adults-only one. Ticketed, worth it, and the Pier 15 crowd is the friendliest in the city.

Friday, 7:15 — a movie under the sky. PROXY's Fall Film Fest in Hayes Valley screens I Love Boosters at 432 Octavia: first-come blankets, food and drink on site. The jets stop at four; this starts at 7:15; it's a ten-minute walk from either.

Friday, 8 PM — Dance Fridays at Space 550. Beginner salsa and bachata lessons at eight sharp, then the floor till late. Nobody arrives with a partner. Showing up alone isn't just normal here, it's the format.

Saturday, 11 — free yoga in the park. Whale's Tail Meadow in Golden Gate Park, near the rose garden. Donations welcome; bring a mat and a layer.

Saturday, 2–4 — Follow the Music. A free afternoon concert back at Yerba Buena Gardens, between the Blue Angels and dinner.

Sunday, noon — Lindy in the Park. Free swing lesson by the de Young, every Sunday since 1996. Thirty years of proof that dancing with strangers works. (YBG Dance Day runs 10–3 the same day if you want to make it a double.)

Every listing above was checked against the organizer this week. Plans change — tap through to the source before you head out.
