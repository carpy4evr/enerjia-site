Content draft — week of 2026-08-11

First real output of runbook step 4. Channel-agnostic on purpose (the newsletter/Instagram/TikTok mix isn't decided yet) — themed picks plus short hooks, so it can flex into whichever format you land on.

Theme this week: the weekday problem. Weekends have never been the issue — Mon/Tue/Wed have. Everything sourced this pass is deliberately a weeknight, which also makes for a genuinely useful post rather than another "here's your weekend" roundup that everyone else already writes.

Newsletter angle — "Your week doesn't have to start on Friday"

Short intro, then the four picks below. The pitch writes itself: most people treat Monday-to-Wednesday as dead time. It isn't — it's just unadvertised.

Monday — Open Mic at Hotel Utah Saloon

One of the longest-running open mics in the city, in a building that's been standing since 1908. Go to perform, or go to watch strangers be brave on a Monday night. 7:00 PM, SoMa.

Tuesday — Trivia Night at The Dubliner

Noe Valley pub quiz with actual regulars and actual competition. Music and picture rounds. Free to play. Turn up early if you want a table. 8:00 PM.

Tuesday — W Run Club

Free group run from the W, out toward the Embarcadero and the Ferry Building. All paces. No membership, no sign-up, no one checking whether you belong. 4:45 PM.

Wednesday — Bar Bingo at The Dubliner

Same pub, different game. Bingo, prizes, and you genuinely don't need to bring anyone — showing up alone is the normal way to do this one. 8:00 PM.

Instagram / TikTok — daily story format

Per the three-a-day structure (morning / midday / end-of-day), same skeleton each time with the text swapped:

  • Morning — "Good morning. Here's what's on today." + the day's pick(s)
  • Midday — "Still time." + the evening pick
  • End of day — "You missed it. Tomorrow:" + tomorrow's pick

The weeknight events above are the strongest material for this — a Monday story with something genuinely on that night is far more useful than another weekend listicle, and it's the exact gap the feed is meant to fill.

Suggested captions

  • Monday: "Open mic at a bar older than the state's freeway system. 7PM, SoMa."
  • Tuesday: "Two options tonight: run it off at 4:45, or out-trivia Noe Valley at 8."
  • Wednesday: "Bar bingo. You don't need a team. You don't need a plan."
