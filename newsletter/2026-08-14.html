Content draft — week of 2026-08-17

Runbook step 4 for sourcing passes 19–21. Channel-agnostic as before (the newsletter/Instagram mix is still undecided) — themed picks plus hooks.

Theme this week: Friday got fixed. Last week's honest answer to "what's happening Friday night that isn't a bar" was: not much. This week there are three answers, all cheap or free, all fine to show up to alone, all running for weeks. That's the story — Enerjia didn't just list Friday, it went and found one.

Newsletter angle — "The Friday problem, solved three ways"

Short intro on the premise: SF weeknights are full of trivia and run clubs, but Friday always defaulted to "get a drink." Not anymore. Then the three picks, in energy order:

Skate it — Friday Night Skate (the Midnight Rollers)

Every Friday since 1989. Gather 8:45 PM under the Ferry Building clock tower, roll at 9 — a ten-mile DJ-soundtracked party through the city and back. Free. Helmet and lights required; rain cancels. You will not be the only first-timer, and nobody skates it alone for long. (No skate Sep 4 — Burning Man week. The one week the organizers take off.)

Draw it, loudly — Communal Table at the Drawing Room

Friday nights 6–10 at a Valencia Street gallery: one long table, supplies provided, draw or collage or do a puzzle or just hang. $5–20 sliding scale, all ages, BYOB. Deliberately structured so strangers end up making things next to each other. (Published dates: Aug 21, 28, Sep 4, 11 — skip weeks happen, check before you go.)

Draw it, quietly — Studio 203 Figure Drawing

Fridays 6–8:45 PM at 1890 Bryant Street Studios: drop-in figure drawing with a live model, $20, all levels, bring your own materials. Two hours of focus with company — the introvert's Friday night out. Saturday 9 AM session too, if your Friday is spoken for.

The Tuesday bonus — Decentered Open Mic

Doesn't fit the Friday theme but too good to sit on: every Tuesday 6–9 PM at 1175 Folsom, a free multi-genre open mic — poetry, standup, music, film. Three-minute slots, sign up in person. Half the room is performers waiting their turn, which is exactly why going alone works.

Save for a Monday/Tuesday send, or the "tomorrow:" end-of-day story slot.

Visual note

The Friday Night Skate is the most filmable event on the map — a rolling light show leaving the Ferry Building at 9 PM. If any event justifies going in person with a phone for story footage, it's this one, this Friday.
