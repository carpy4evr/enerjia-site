Content draft — week of 2026-08-24

Runbook step 4 for passes 22–26 (five passes, one day). Channel-agnostic as always: newsletter section, story skeleton, captions.

Theme this week: Saturday assembles itself. For once the pitch isn't one great pick — it's that this Saturday, August 22, a person could run the whole day on the map without a single reservation. That's the product demo as the newsletter.

Newsletter angle — "A Saturday, fully assembled"

Walk the day chronologically; let the density do the arguing:

9:30 AM — a 5k on JFK Promenade

Founder Run Club's birthday run through Golden Gate Park. All paces, zero membership. (Or Marina Green at 10 for Girls Run + Brunch — run, then a table you didn't have to book.)

11 AM — free yoga in the meadow

Whale's Tail Meadow, near the rose garden. Free, donations welcome, bring a mat and layers. An hour of stretch before the afternoon gets loud.

1–6 PM — an inaugural block party

The Guardsmen close Chestnut Street between Pierce & Divisadero for their first-ever Marina Block Party: two live bands, family-friendly, free. First editions of things become traditions — be able to say you were at this one.

Evening — pick your energy

Create for Good at the Palace of Fine Arts (5:30, making things for good causes), or rest up: the Friday Night Skate did ten miles the night before, and Sunday morning has plans for you.

Sunday coda — swing in the park

Lindy in the Park, every Sunday since 1996, free lesson at noon by the de Young. Thirty years of proof that dancing with strangers works.
