How it's different

The events are public — a street festival is a street festival. What's yours is the room: when you join an event, your chat is with the women going, not the whole crowd. "Anyone want to walk over together from BART?" lands differently when you know who's reading it.

It's the same verified calendar as everything on Enerjia. Nothing is hidden from you; a space is added for you.

The honest fine print

Membership is self-attested — you tell us this space is for you, and we believe you. It is not identity-verified yet (a verification option is on the roadmap), and it is actively moderated: reporting and blocking work inside every room, and reports are reviewed by a human. We'd rather tell you exactly what the promise is than oversell it.
