Friday Night Skate — the Midnight Rollers

Ten miles through the city on wheels, DJ-soundtracked, every Friday since 1989. Free. Helmet and lights required. Nobody skates it alone for long.

One long table at a Mission gallery — draw, collage, or just hang. Supplies provided, $5–20 sliding scale, all ages. Built for strangers to make things next to each other.

How it works

Open the map. Everything near you, tonight through next week, with real times and real addresses — no ticket-site junk, no phantom listings. Every event is checked against the organizer who runs it before it earns a pin.

Joining an event unlocks its group chat, so "is anyone else going?" has an answer before you leave the house. That's the whole trick: the hard part of going out alone was never the event — it was the first message.
